## Scope

Whole app shell and every screen of «I+D · Cuaderno de cocina» (web/PWA + Electron), mode: Operate (Read for Biblioteca entries). Redesign: visual world replaced; features, data, routes and copy kept.

## Audience and task

One professional chef (Ibiza, mediterranean embers). Phone in the kitchen between services and PC at home, equally. Priorities: 1) Platos (create, test, refine), 2) Biblioteca and learning, 3) Laboratorio and ideas. Diario, Trayectoria, Entrenar, Herramientas, Mi cocina second tier.

## Chosen direction

Lámina de laboratorio — the plates of modern scientific cookery, chosen by the user («Elige tú» → assigned direction).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The chef's R&D seen as real science plates: dishes drawn as cross-sections, temperatures on a measured thermal scale, numbers as data. Refuses the cream-paper-plus-serif recipe notebook and the photo-card recipe app.

OWN-WORLD: Carbon ground (#111214) with graphite panels, chalk-white ink, steel greys; one-pixel rules everywhere, square corners, no drop shadows. Colour does jobs: a thermal scale (cold blue → teal → amber → fire red) for temperature, state and selection; fire red marks change and the primary action. Barlow for reading and UI, Barlow Condensed for titles and data labels, tabular figures always. A light «lámina blanca» variant for bright kitchens keeps the same system.

STORY: The chef opens the app and sees at once what is cooking (a dish in progress drawn as layers), what to learn today, and where everything is; numbers (grams, degrees, minutes, scores) read like lab data.

FIRST VIEWPORT: PC: left rail 240 px on carbon, Hoy then Platos, Biblioteca, Laboratorio in large condensed type, then a second tier (Entrenar, Diario, Trayectoria) and a third (Sabores, Temporada, Radar, Herramientas, Preguntar, Mi cocina). Main: date and greeting over a one-pixel rule, primary action «Crear un plato» top right; dish in progress as a cross-section panel; «Para hoy» list; entry of the day with a thermal scale. Phone: bottom bar Hoy, Platos, Crear (centre), Biblioteca, Laboratorio; the rest in «Más».

FORM: Assigned grounded direction 7 of 7 (food-science plates), raised by: one-pixel rule discipline (centre-rail reference), density courage (Japanese high-density web), single time axis (labanotation), change stays marked until seen (boarding pass), tabular aligned figures (datamatics). seed:556919a9312c083a

SIGNATURE: the cross-section — every dish's components drawn as stacked layers by role with their weights; temperatures plotted on the thermal scale.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
